What we tested

Unlimited original quality, tested.

The unofficial Pixel XL upload mode currently works without using account storage in our tests. On 16 October 2026, Google Photos marked a photo and video uploaded from each platform as Original quality and not taking up account storage. Downloaded originals matched their source SHA-256 hashes.

Mac albums and Windows folders kept their source names and expected contents. These findings apply to those fixtures and account only: Google can change sign-in, uploads, or storage charging at any time.

Details

Good to know.

Does this free up storage on my device?

No. It creates copies in Google Photos and leaves your original files where they are.

Are album names kept?

macOS can recreate selected Apple Photos albums and Windows can mirror folder names. Android mirrors accessible device-folder names; those folders are not logical Google albums.

Can it reclaim iCloud storage?

iCloud backup is copy-only by default. Experimental reclaim requires a reviewed manual preview or explicit automatic confirmation of up to 100 items, then fresh Google original readback and SHA-256 verification for every component before moving an item to Recently Deleted. It does not promise immediate free space or a permanent purge. Live deletion/recovery validation is still pending.

Is it an official Google app?

No. It uses the unofficial gpmc client. Google may change authentication, upload behaviour, or storage accounting.
